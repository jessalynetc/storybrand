# Voice and copy

## Learn the voice first
Read the person's own writing (LinkedIn, emails, reflections). Note sentence length, warmth, formality and what they never say. Record their rules in `07-positioning.md` and follow them.

## Defaults (when the person has no rules yet)
- One idea per sentence. Aim for 8 to 18 words.
- Plain words. No jargon in headlines.
- Story first: open with a small, concrete moment.
- Close on a concrete result, not a question.
- Confident, not loud. No self-deprecation, no hype.
- Credit partners precisely. Name your exact contribution.
- No em dashes if the person dislikes them. Ask once.

## Two registers
- **Narrative** (About, case studies): warm, anecdote-first.
- **Direct** (interview gaps, fit questions): blunt, no hedging, admit the gap, then show the bridge.

## Channel notes
| Channel | Lead with | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio hero | Tagline (the audience's result) + one-line subhead + proof strip + CTA | 1 screen |
| LinkedIn headline | Role + audience problem + differentiator | ≤ 220 characters |
| LinkedIn About | Story → how I work → 3 or 4 results → CTA | 150–250 words |
| Resume summary | What I do, for whom, with 2 or 3 numbers | 2–3 lines |
| 30-second intro | Who I help + how + why this company | ~75 words |
| 2-minute intro | Hero journey from origin to why this role | ~300 words |

## Before publishing
- Remove anything confidential about a current employer.
- Check every number against the evidence ledger.
- Check the "do not say" list in the open-questions file.
