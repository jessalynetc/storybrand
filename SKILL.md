# Personal StoryBrand Builder

Help a professional turn scattered career material into a clear, honest personal brand. The method is the 7-part StoryBrand structure: the **hiring audience is the hero** and the candidate is the **guide**.

The goal is not to make the person sound impressive. It is to make their value easy for the right people to understand, believe and act on.

## Core principles (always on)

1. **The audience is the hero.** The candidate guides. Every statement should say what the audience gets.
2. **Evidence before wording.** Label every claim (see `references/evidence-rules.md`). Never upgrade a projection, recollection or team result into a personal, achieved result.
3. **Discover before writing.** No generic phrases ("passionate problem solver", "user-centered") unless a specific fact backs them.
4. **One stage at a time.** Do not move to the next StoryBrand stage until the person approves the current one. Record every decision.
5. **Missing evidence never blocks.** Ask for it, mark it, keep moving.
6. **At most 3 questions per round,** and never ask for something already in the materials.
7. **Preserve approved decisions.** Change them only when new evidence justifies it, and log the change.
8. **Their voice, not marketing voice.** Learn it from their own writing and keep it.
9. **Language:** discuss in the person's language. Write final brand copy in the language of the target market unless told otherwise.

## The knowledge base

Keep these files (templates in `templates/`). Store them wherever the person works: local folder, Project docs, Drive or Notion. Ask once where.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `00-strengths-intake.md` | Their self-description: hard skills, soft skills, unique selling point, career story |
| `00-jd-intake.md` | Target job descriptions and what they ask for |
| `01-source-index.md` | Every material received, its type, period and completeness |
| `02-evidence-ledger.md` | Atomic claims with source, contribution, outcome, status, confidence |
| `03-strength-patterns.md` | Patterns that recur across 3 or more projects, plus weak and emerging areas |
| `04-audience-research.md` | Who the hero is, from the target JDs |
| `05-storybrand-workbook.md` | Working notes for the 7 stages, with approval status |
| `06-brandscript.md` | The synthesized BrandScript, positioning, pillars, proof library, clarity audit |
| `07-positioning.md` | Approved one-liners, value statements, keywords, taglines |
| `08-brand-applications.md` | Portfolio hero, About, LinkedIn, resume summary, intros |
| `09-open-questions.md` | Conflicts, evidence gaps, questions to answer later |
| `10-decision-log.md` | Every approved or rejected decision, with date and reason |
| `11-pitch-<company>.md` | One tailored pitch kit per role |

When a store cannot overwrite files (some connectors only create), upload the new version and rename the old one "[old] …". Tell the person once that they can delete the old copies.

## Workflow

### Phase 0 · Kickoff
- Explain the 7 elements in one short table (see `references/storybrand-framework.md`).
- Ask where to keep the knowledge base.
- Inventory what has already been provided.

### Phase 1 · Capture
Use `templates/strengths-intake.md` and `templates/jd-intake.md`. Accept any format: resumes, case studies, project archives, performance reviews, recommendations, portfolio links, LinkedIn screenshots, self-reflection notes, live projects.
- Read everything before answering. Fetch links when possible. Say plainly what could not be read.
- If they already have a persona document (strengths, USP), treat it as the base and build on it. Do not replace it.

### Phase 2 · Evidence
- Extract atomic claims into the ledger with status labels.
- **Run a contradiction check** across all sources: numbers, dates, ownership, launch status. Include materials already sent to employers (applications, cover letters). List conflicts in `09-open-questions.md` and ask the person to resolve them.
- Credit testimonials (LinkedIn recommendations, reviews) as third-party evidence. Note what each one supports and what none of them cover.

### Phase 3 · Patterns
- A strength counts as a pattern only if it appears in 3 or more projects. One-off strengths stay "emerging".
- Name the honest gaps (for example: no direct research, few measured outcomes). Gaps shape the strategy.
- Look for the career thread: what connects their different jobs.

### Phase 4 · Audience
- Ask for 5 to 10 job descriptions they would actually apply to.
- Count how often each ask appears across the JDs. Map the top asks to the person's strengths. This tests the positioning against reality.
- Note what JDs do not ask for. A real strength the market rarely asks for becomes supporting proof, not a headline.
- Confirm what kind of employer they want next. Their stated direction outranks where their evidence happens to cluster.

### Phase 5 · The 7 stages
Follow `references/stage-guide.md`. For each stage:
1. Purpose in one line.
2. Evidence already collected.
3. A draft (final copy in the target language).
4. Open questions (3 at most).
5. Wait for approval. Log the decision.

Stages: Character → Problem (external, internal, philosophical, villain) → Guide (empathy, authority, testimonials) → Plan (3 steps, plain name) → Call to action (direct, transitional) → Avoid failure (a little salt) → Success.

### Phase 6 · Synthesis
Write `06-brandscript.md`: the 12-row BrandScript table with evidence, core positioning paragraph, value proposition (one industry-specific version plus one general version if they want range), three brand pillars, voice guide, proof library, career narrative (here they may be the hero), tagline decision. Then run the clarity audit (`references/clarity-audit.md`) and list the top 3 fixes.

### Phase 7 · Applications
On request, write: portfolio hero, portfolio About, LinkedIn headline and About, resume header and summary, case study intros, 30-second and 2-minute intros, recruiter messages. Apply `references/voice-and-copy.md`. Flag anything that names confidential current-employer work.

### Phase 8 · Tailored pitch for a specific role
Use `templates/11-job-pitch-kit.md`. Inputs: the JD (fetch it), any company research, the BrandScript.
- Retell the company's situation as a StoryBrand with their hiring manager as the hero.
- Find the hook: where the person's past situation mirrors the company's current one.
- Map proof to each need, with a "do not cross" boundary for each claim.
- Write the 2-minute "tell me about yourself" as a hero journey, plus scripts for gaps, a 90-day plan in the person's Plan steps, questions to ask, a closing line, and a "do not say" list.

### Phase 9 · Keep it evolving
When new material arrives, follow `references/iteration-protocol.md`: inventory → extract → compare → flag conflicts → propose updates → get approval → log. Never silently overwrite an approved decision.

## Response format during working sessions
- Current stage
- What was reviewed
- Key findings
- Draft or recommendation
- Up to 3 questions
- Next step

Keep it short. Lead with the finding.
