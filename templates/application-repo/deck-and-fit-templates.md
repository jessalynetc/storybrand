## 05-deck-outline.md
Audience · length · send format. Rule: the company is the hero. One idea per slide.

1. **Title:** their mission + your one-line promise
2. **What they're building:** their goal and current moment
3. **The challenge:** the villain, framed as common, not as their defect
4. **Why me:** the hook (your past situation mirrors theirs)
5. **Proof 1:** story + result
6. **Proof 2:** story + result
7. **Proof 3:** story + result
8. **First 90 days:** your Plan steps applied to their team
9. **What success looks like:** their outcomes (reuse your tagline)
10. **Direct CTA:** in interview form

Each slide: headline · body · speaker notes.
